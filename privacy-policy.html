IntelliDoc Privacy Policy

Your paper, filed and safe — and always on your device first.

Effective August 30, 2026
The short version: IntelliDoc keeps your scans, OCR text, transcriptions, redactions and signatures on your iPhone. We never upload document contents unless you explicitly use iCloud Sync, backup, a share link, or an AI action — and then only the minimum data needed. No ads. No data sales. No tracking.

01Information We Collect

Stored only on your device

The following never leaves your device unless you export, share, or enable sync for it:

  • Scanned page images and generated PDF documents
  • OCR text extracted from your documents
  • Handwriting transcription text
  • Redaction records and signature profiles
  • Document titles, notes, folders, tags and keywords
  • App settings and preferences

Optional iCloud sync

If you enable iCloud Sync, your vault syncs between your own Apple devices through Apple's iCloud in the app's private iCloud Drive container. This data is governed by Apple's privacy policy. We have no access to the contents of your iCloud Drive.

Optional metadata backup (Firebase)

If you enable cloud backup, a minimal metadata record may be stored with Google Firebase (Firestore / Cloud Storage): a randomly generated identifier (never your name, email or Apple ID), document titles, folder names, page counts, timestamps and sync bookkeeping. We do not upload document images, OCR text, transcriptions or signatures to Firebase. Disabling backup or using Settings → Manage Data → Delete Account & Data removes these records.

AI features (optional, explicit)

If you explicitly run an AI feature (rewriting or summarizing selected text), only the text you selected is sent through our secured gateway to a large-language-model provider (Google Gemini). It is processed solely to return your result, is not used to train models, and is discarded after the response. No document images are ever sent.

What we never collect

  • No advertising identifiers — and no ads, ever.
  • No third-party analytics that profile you; only anonymous technical events with no document content.
  • No contacts, location or microphone data unless a feature requires it in the moment — and we don't retain it.

03Share Links

If you create a secure share link, the document is uploaded over an encrypted connection and accessible only via the generated link. Links expire automatically (default 7 days) and you can revoke them anytime by deleting the share in the app — deletion removes the stored file.

04Subscriptions

Purchases and subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We never see your payment details; Apple provides only an anonymous receipt to validate entitlements.

05Legal Bases (EEA / UK)

Where the GDPR applies: on-device data is yours and stays under your control. Optional cloud backup and AI processing rely on your consent, which you may withdraw anytime by disabling the feature or deleting your data. We do not transfer personal data to third parties for their own use.

06Your Rights

Access & export — export all user-created data as a portable archive from Settings → Manage Data.
Erasure — permanently erase all device and cloud copies via Delete Account & Data. Deletion is immediate and irreversible.
Withdraw consent — disable iCloud Sync, backup or AI features anytime in Settings.
GDPR / CCPA requests — because we keep no profile database of personal information, on-device export and deletion fulfill access and deletion requests. Anything else: atkinsmedia.io/support.

07Children's Privacy

IntelliDoc is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because document data stays on-device, no child data reaches us.

08Data Retention

  • Device data — retained until you delete it or uninstall the app (except iCloud data when sync is enabled).
  • Backup metadata — retained until you delete your account data or disable backup.
  • Share links — files deleted automatically when the link expires or is revoked.

09Security

Documents are stored in the iOS app sandbox with iOS data protection. Network features use TLS, and share links use unguessable identifiers with expiration. Our architecture minimizes risk by keeping sensitive data on your device.
